# htve

Exactly what every Hotwire customer needs -- all your channels in one click

I've been a Hotwire cable customer for a while now and never realized how many streaming channels were included with my subscription until I installed this extension. Having 90+ TV Everywhere channels organized right in my browser toolbar is a game-changer. No more Googling "how to watch ESPN with Hotwire" or bookmarking a dozen different network sites.

The search bar is fast and finds channels instantly -- just start typing and it narrows things down. I also love the platform filters so I can quickly see which channels support web streaming vs. mobile only. The favorites feature is the cherry on top; I starred my go-to channels (ESPN, CNN, HGTV) and now they always float to the top of the list. One click and I'm watching live TV.

The extension is lightweight, clean, and doesn't ask for unnecessary permissions. It just does what it says and does it well. If you're a Hotwire subscriber, stop sleeping on your TV Everywhere benefits -- this extension makes it effortless to actually use them.
